# Olympic Games Historical Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze historical Olympic Games datasets, tracking overall medal tallies, participating athletes, demographic breakdowns, and top-performing nations and individual athletes.

## 📋 Project Overview

This Power BI project provides an end-to-end data analytics solution for examining historical performance across the Olympic Games. It visualizes high-level summary KPIs alongside granular breakdowns by sport, nation, age group, and gender to reveal key trends in Olympic competition history.

## 🛠️ Dashboard Features & Key Insights

* **KPI Scorecards**: Top-level indicators summarizing total participation and performance:
  * **Total Medals**: 39,783
  * **Gold Medals**: 13,372
  * **Silver Medals**: 13,116
  * **Bronze Medals**: 13,295
  * **Total Athletes**: 135,571
* **Geographic & Sport Analysis**:
  * **Country Map**: Visual representation of participating nations and medal distribution.
  * **Top Sports**: Ranking of top sports by volume (Athletics, Swimming, Cycling, Shooting, Gymnastics).
* **Demographic Breakdown**:
  * **Age Buckets**: Segmentation of medalists across age groups (*Bet 20 & 30*, *Bet 30 & 50*, *Under 20*, *Above 50*).
  * **Gender Distribution**: Breakdown of Gold medals won by female (F) and male (M) athletes across leading countries.
* **Leaderboards & Country Rankings**:
  * **Medals by Country**: Breakdown of Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals for top nations (United States, Soviet Union, Germany, Great Britain, France).
  * **Top Athletes Leaderboard**: Itemized table tracking individual medal counts, featuring leading record holders like Michael Phelps (28 medals) and Larysa Latynina (18 medals).
* **Interactive Slicers**: Filtering panel on the left navigation bar enabling drill-downs by **Year**, **Team**, **Medal**, **Sex**, **Sport**, and **Age Bucket**.
